PLAINLY SPEAKING

Privacy & account deletion

This development build stores account identifiers, password hashes, purchase and return records, refund transactions, activity history and sessions on its configured server. The mobile app uses platform secure storage for credentials and local SQLite for purchase drafts.

The account export above retrieves the data provided by the server’s export operation. Account deletion is available above and inside the mobile app. Deletion removes active database records; server backup retention and any future object-storage retention must be documented by the operator before release.

Draft disclosure only. The operator’s legal identity, contact, retention periods, production subprocessors and jurisdiction-specific terms remain unconfigured. This page is not an approved public privacy policy or legal terms.

Terms

Return deadlines and receipt of funds are confirmed by the user. This build does not integrate with banks, retailers or live carrier tracking, and does not guarantee return eligibility, delivery or refunds.

Public legal terms require operator review before release.
